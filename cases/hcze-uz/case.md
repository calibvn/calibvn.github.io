## Project overview

A website for HANGCHA’s representative office in Uzbekistan, presenting warehouse and material-handling equipment.

Developer.

Develop an equipment catalogue website for presenting the product range and collecting commercial enquiries.

## What I delivered

- Implemented the company and equipment category presentation.
- Created product pages and contact forms.
- Built the website with WordPress and Elementor.
