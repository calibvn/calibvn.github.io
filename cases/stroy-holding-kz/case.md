# Stroy Holding

## Project overview

A corporate website for a supplier of construction materials, paints, and coatings. It presents product categories, services, company information, and an industry blog.

## My role

Project manager.

## Objective

Organise the development of a corporate website that presents the company’s product range and services and collects enquiries.

## What I delivered

- Coordinated the website structure and development on 1C-Bitrix.
- Delivered product category, service, company, and blog sections.
- Oversaw the implementation of enquiry forms.
- Delivered the Bitrix24 integration.

1 UX/UI-designer  
1 frontend developer  
1 backend developer

## Technologies

1C-Bitrix, Bitrix24.

## Result

Launched a corporate website for presenting the company’s products and generating enquiries.
